# Acknowledgements

A book that tries to be current in a market this fast-moving is never the work of one person, and this one owes debts on every page.

To the **institutions that make the Indian derivatives market transparent** — the National Stock Exchange, BSE, the Securities and Exchange Board of India, and the clearing corporations — whose circulars, contract specifications, and public studies are the factual backbone of this book. SEBI's willingness to publish hard data on retail F&O outcomes, in particular, gave this book its moral centre: you cannot teach people to survive a game whose odds are hidden.

To the **practitioners** — the market makers, proprietary traders, risk managers, and quantitative analysts who trade Indian index options for a living — whose willingness to explain how margin *really* moves on an expiry-day spike, how a book is actually hedged, and where accounts *actually* blow up shaped the parts of this book that no textbook could. The chapters on margin management, portfolio Greeks in practice, and the anatomy of a blow-up exist because professionals were generous with the truth.

To the **educators and authors who built the foundations** — the derivatives theorists whose models we still price with, and the trading writers who first taught the discipline of position sizing, expectancy, and process over outcome. This book stands on that work and tries to translate it faithfully into the Indian context.

To the **NISM** and the professional-certification community, whose curricula set the standard for what a competent Indian derivatives professional should know, and to the many broker research desks and analytics platforms whose tools make the option chain, the Greeks, and the volatility surface visible to ordinary traders.

To the **reviewers** who read these chapters as a professional options trader, a quantitative analyst, a risk manager, a derivatives trainer, and a technical editor — and who checked the market-specific facts against live exchange and SEBI sources. Their scrutiny caught errors of arithmetic, of currency, and of emphasis; those that remain are the author's own.

And to the **reader who is about to risk real capital in a market where most participants lose** — this book was written for you, and with a genuine sense of responsibility toward the money you will put at stake. Read it carefully, verify every time-sensitive number before you act, and never let the wish to be right override the discipline to survive.

Finally, a standing reminder that is itself an acknowledgement: **rules, rates, lot sizes, and tax law in India change every year.** Where this book gives a specific figure, it reflects the position in 2026 and the sources available then. Always confirm the current position with SEBI, your exchange, and a qualified Chartered Accountant before you trade or file. The debt this book owes to *tomorrow's* circulars is one only the reader can settle.
